JEP4X8 · PLAYER PRIVACY

policy.

EFFECTIVE 22 OCTOBER 2026

This single policy applies to all mobile games and related services published by Jep4x8. Features and service integrations can differ by app, region, and version, so review the Data Safety information on the relevant Google Play listing for the latest app-specific disclosure.

Information and game data

Current Google Play Data Safety disclosures report that the games published by Jep4x8 do not collect data. Sharing disclosures vary by app: some listings report no data shared, while others report that location, app activity, and app info and performance may be shared with third parties. These disclosures are provided by the publisher and may vary by app, region, and version. Check the Data Safety section of each Google Play listing for the latest app-specific information.

Where a game stores settings or progress locally, that information remains on your device. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it will generally remove locally stored information, subject to your device and account settings.

Third-party services

Depending on the app and version, games may include third-party platform, advertising, analytics, or diagnostic components. Services that may be used include Google Play Services, AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Meta, Unity, AppLovin, and ironSource; not every service is used by every game. Some services may receive or process information as described in the applicable Google Play Data Safety disclosure and the provider’s privacy policy. If an app’s data practices change, this policy and the relevant Play disclosure will be updated.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising and AdMob
  • Firebase privacy and security
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • ironSource Privacy Policy

Children

Age ratings and audience information can differ between games and are shown on each Google Play listing. Jep4x8 does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information can contact Jep4x8 using the address below.

Retention and your choices

Locally stored game information can generally be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the game. If a future feature collects or shares information, the applicable retention and deletion options will be explained in this policy and the relevant Google Play disclosure. Information handled by a third-party provider is subject to that provider’s own retention and request procedures. You can contact Jep4x8 with a privacy question or request.

Security

Google Play’s current Data Safety disclosures report that data is encrypted in transit for games published by Jep4x8. These disclosures can change with an app, region, or version. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Links and this website

Games and this website may link to third-party services, including Google Play. Those services are not controlled by Jep4x8, and their own privacy policies apply. This presentation website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, or sign-in cookies. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, for delivery and security. When this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when a game or its services change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when this version was published. Please review it from time to time, and check the relevant Google Play listing for app-specific disclosures.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact the publisher: jep4x8@hotmail.com.

This is a general privacy policy for all games and related services published by Jep4x8; it does not refer to any one game. The statements above reflect current Google Play disclosures and should be reviewed whenever an app, SDK, or data practice changes.
